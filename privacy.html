Sprout

Privacy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Sprout is a meal-planning tool. This notice describes information the app handles when you use it. The person or organization operating the Firebase project and website is responsible for publishing accurate contact and legal information.

Before launch: replace this notice with a reviewed policy that names the operator and provides a monitored privacy contact. This template is not legal advice.

Information you enter

Your planner may store your age, height, weight, activity, food preference, goals, meal schedule, allergy notes, meal entries, nutrition values, hydration counts, and selected dates. If you create an account, Firebase Authentication handles your sign-in details and the planner associates saved data with your Firebase user ID.

Where information is stored

The app saves planner data in this browser's local storage so it remains available on that device. If the operator configures Firebase and you sign in, the app also saves planner data in that operator's Cloud Firestore project under your account. Guest plans remain in the browser. Local data can be removed by clearing this site's browser storage; that does not delete a copy already stored in Firestore.

Firebase Authentication and Firestore are Google services. When enabled, the app loads Firebase's web SDK from Google's CDN. Google and any sign-in provider you choose may process technical and account information under their own terms and privacy notices. Sprout currently includes no advertising or analytics code.

Use, deletion, and sharing

Planner information is used to show and save your plan. The app does not intentionally sell or share planner information for advertising. The Firebase project operator can access and administer data in that project. This version has no in-app account or cloud-data deletion feature; ask the project operator to remove account data. Review and update this section if the app's hosting, services, or features change.

Food and health information

Profile and allergy notes can be sensitive. Enter only information you are comfortable storing in your browser and, if enabled, in the configured Firebase project. Meal and calorie suggestions are estimates, are not medical advice, and cannot establish that food is safe for an allergy.
